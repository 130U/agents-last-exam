# Decision addendum — count, taxonomy and private-benchmark hook

## Decisions adopted in Draft v2

1. Base acceptance unit: 1,000 distinct workflow packages, not 1,000 prompts or cosmetic variants.
2. Minimum package: one canonical runnable instance plus the full task/environment/reference/evaluator/QA contract.
3. Extra variants: separate count, acceptance and commercial manifest; no ALE-ratio extrapolation.
4. Taxonomy wording: 13 formal domains and 55 subdomains, with the v2-only `Other → Sports` display caveat.
5. Hook: explain why a client buys private measurement credibility and why ALE is a partial architectural response rather than a cure.

## Numeric audit

- `960 workflows` and `960 external submissions` remain separate units.
- `1,490 = 150 public + 1,017 private + 323 pending QC`.
- Public/all inventory: `150 / 1,490 = 10.07%`.
- Public/(public+private): `150 / 1,167 = 12.85%`.
- Neither denominator supports “about 20% public.”

## Open decisions

Intended use, domain/region/language priorities, rights isolation, environment/network/retention constraints, human baseline, run and maintenance budgets, owners, pilot losses/stops and delivery-service boundaries remain unconfirmed.
