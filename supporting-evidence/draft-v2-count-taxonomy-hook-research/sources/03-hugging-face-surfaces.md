## Short quotation

> “55 subfields grouped into 13 industry clusters”

## Evidence extracted

- The paper page repeats the 13-cluster/55-subfield headline.
- The fixed dataset is a 153-row metadata-only public release.
- Its structured metadata exposes 14 domain codes including `other/sports`, but only 51 observed structured subdomain mappings.

## Boundary

The 14 observed storage/taxonomy codes are not proof of 14 stable formal top-level domains. The public metadata subset cannot independently reconstruct the full 55-item taxonomy or the private runnable inventory.
