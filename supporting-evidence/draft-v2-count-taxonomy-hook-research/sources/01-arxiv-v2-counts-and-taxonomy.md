- URL: https://arxiv.org/html/2606.05405v2
- Revision: arXiv `2606.05405v2`, 2026-06-11
- Type: primary paper

## Short quotation

> “The current ALE release contains 960 task workflows and 1,490 task instances in total.”

## Evidence extracted

- Figure 5: 960 external submissions + 530 commissioned tasks.
- Figure 5: 150 public + 1,017 private + 323 unverified pending QC.
- Figure 2 caption: 55 subdomains grouped under 13 top-level domains.
- Figure 2 display: 13 named domain bands plus `Other → Sports`.
- Appendix terminology distinguishes workflows from concrete runnable instances.

## Boundaries

- The two 960s are different ledgers; the paper publishes no row-level crosswalk.
- 1,490 includes pending-QC inventory and is not a final-accepted count.
- The paper does not define `Other` as a 14th formal domain.
