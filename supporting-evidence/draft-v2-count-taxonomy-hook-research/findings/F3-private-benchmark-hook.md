# F3 — The hook should explain the customer's purchase logic

## Finding

The hook should move through five links: public-score misalignment → exposure and targeted optimization → customer demand for controlled private measurement → ALE's architectural response → residual risks and project design consequences.

## Safe claim

ALE converts several benchmaxxing failure modes into stronger engineering and governance controls.

## Unsafe claim

ALE solves benchmaxxing or proves its private pool is uncontaminated.

## Commercial inference

The customer buys measurement credibility—controlled access, hidden scoring assets, reproducible execution and rotation—not merely scarce question text.
