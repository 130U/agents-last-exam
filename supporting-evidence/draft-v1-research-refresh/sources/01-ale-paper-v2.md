# Source 01 — Agents' Last Exam paper v2

- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05405v2
- **Surface:** arXiv v2, 2026-06-11
- **Role:** primary, version-pinned design and inventory evidence
- **Accessed:** 2026-08-09

> “The current ALE release contains 960 task workflows and 1,490 task instances in total.”

## Supports

- Workflow and instance are different units.
- Figure 5 reports 150 public, 1,017 private, and 323 pending-QC instances; 1,490 is therefore not an all-accepted count.
- Task production uses staged expert sourcing, submission, review, engineering, and final QC.
- A mostly private pool and rolling release are design controls against targeted optimization.

## Limits

- Figure 5's 960 external submissions and the paper's 960 workflows are not interchangeable; no row-level crosswalk is published.
- Repeat-run evidence is configuration-specific and is not a universal confidence-interval protocol.
- The paper does not provide the throughput, cost, staffing, yield, or matched-human data needed to price this project.

## Source assessment

High credibility for version-specific facts; construct-validity claims remain first-party claims.
