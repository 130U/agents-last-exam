> “fail to accurately quantify uncertainty”

## Supports

An evaluation must distinguish fixed-benchmark performance from performance generalized to a broader task universe. Those estimands require different assumptions and uncertainty methods. Repeated trials and additional task instances answer different variance questions.

## Limits

No one statistical model is universally appropriate. ALE-style clustered workflows, heterogeneous evaluators, bounded scores, and configuration interactions require a pre-specified analysis matched to the decision.

## Source assessment

High institutional credibility; methodological choice remains project-specific.
