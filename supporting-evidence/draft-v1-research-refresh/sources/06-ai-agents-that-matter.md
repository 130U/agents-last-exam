## Supports

Agent results depend on configuration, elicitation, tools, budget, retries, and cost. Holdouts and intended-use statements matter because developers and downstream buyers make different decisions from the same evaluation.

## Limits

This is a methodological critique, not a 1,000-asset production SOP.

## Source assessment

Medium-high credibility; general principles require adaptation to professional workflow benchmarks.
