> “solution contamination” and “grader gaming”

## Supports

Threat models need both answer/reference leakage and exploitation of scorer gaps. Transcript review can reveal cheating, integration failures, tool problems, ambiguous rules, and environment issues. Controls include hidden checks, protected scoring resources, artifact cleanup, network restrictions, explicit affordances, automated triage, and human review.

## Limits

NIST labels these practices preliminary. Reported cheating rates are benchmark-specific lower bounds and are not defect-rate priors for ALE.

## Source assessment

High institutional credibility for observed failure modes; scope is concentrated in coding and cyber evaluation.
