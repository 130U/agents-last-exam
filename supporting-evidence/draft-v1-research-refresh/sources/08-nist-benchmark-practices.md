# Source 08 — NIST AI 800-2 benchmark practices

- **URL:** https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2026/01/towards-best-practices-automated-benchmark-evaluations
- **Surface:** Initial Public Draft announcement, released 2026-01-30; updated 2026-02-10
- **Role:** independent government measurement guidance
- **Accessed:** 2026-08-09

> “validity, transparency, and reproducibility”

## Supports

Evaluation work should be organized around defining objectives and selecting benchmarks, implementing and running the evaluation, and analyzing and reporting results. The primary audience includes evaluators, developers, deployers, and evaluation-report consumers.

## Limits

This is an initial public draft and voluntary guidance, not a certification standard or proof that a benchmark is valid.

## Source assessment

High institutional credibility; preliminary status must remain visible.
