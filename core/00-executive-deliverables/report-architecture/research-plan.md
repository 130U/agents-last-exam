# Final-report synthesis plan

## Objective

Produce an internal, high-density written decision report that answers both parts of the interview assignment:

1. What ALE measures and how to lead a program that produces 1,000 ALE-style assets.
2. One original worked task that demonstrates the proposed asset contract, evaluator logic, and QA process.

## Method

1. Freeze and reconcile the five completed research packages.
2. Use the Benchmaxxing talk only as a short problem-framing hook.
3. Organize the report by the validity and production decision chain, not by research question number.
4. Put count/version detail, benchmark matrices, corpus inventories, schemas, and full checklists in appendices.
5. Keep paper/code facts, author claims, researcher inference, recommendations, and open decisions visibly separate.
6. Run an adversarial review before finalization: unit audit, evaluator/claim audit, numerical provenance audit, and implementation-feasibility audit.

## Current maturity

- Chapters 1-5: evidence-rich and ready for drafting.
- Chapters 6-9: design principles are clear, but implementation commitments require additional research and/or a pilot.
- Worked original task: not yet selected or built; it must be present in the final deliverable because the interviewer explicitly asked for an example as well as the 1,000-asset leadership plan.

## Output stack

- Main decision report: compact argument and recommendation.
- Technical appendices: version ledger, corpus evidence, benchmark landscape, schemas, rubric, SOP, QA tests, and formulas.
- Optional machine-readable package: source ledger, task schema, acceptance checklist, and pilot measurement sheet.

## Quality gates

- Every number has a surface, revision, date, unit, and evidence role.
- No public count is reused as a production quota.
- No evaluator result is generalized beyond the configured agent system.
- No cost, schedule, staffing, split, pass-rate, or allocation point estimate appears without owner input or pilot evidence.
- The hook contains no more than one short quotation and is not used as independent proof.
- The executive summary states what is decided, what is recommended, and what remains open.
